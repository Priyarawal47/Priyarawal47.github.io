Bombay Shaving Company

Started as an intern. Left leading a design team.

I joined Bombay Shaving Company as an intern and, somewhere between endless artboards, packaging files and campaign deadlines, grew into a Senior Communication Designer.

Over three years, I went from making things to owning how things got made - leading a team of three designers and working across social, e-commerce, web, packaging, print and offline campaigns.

It was fast, messy, hands-on and probably where my habit of obsessing over the tiny details began.

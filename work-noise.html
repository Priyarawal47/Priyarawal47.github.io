Noise

Building launches, not just assets.

At Noise, I worked as a core member of the launch team, shaping the visual direction and execution of some of the brand’s key product launches: from early-stage concepts through to final campaign delivery.

My role involved bringing together multiple creative disciplines across design, 3D, video, UI/UX, photography and production, while collaborating closely with cross-functional teams, external agencies and freelancers.

Alongside hands-on design, I reviewed creative output, mentored junior designers and helped maintain consistency across touchpoints—ensuring that every part of a launch contributed to one cohesive brand story.

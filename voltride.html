Brand Identity

VoltRide

(Concept · EV ride-sharing platform)

A complete identity for an electric ride-sharing platform built around speed, sustainability and smart mobility. The system runs from wordmark and app icon through to a full palette — Storm Surge, Eco Glow, Electric Azure — a Neue Haas Grotesk type stack, an icon set, and the app store presence. Emission-free, and designed to look like the future rather than a compromise.
